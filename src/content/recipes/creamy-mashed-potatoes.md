---
title: "Creamy Mashed Potatoes"
description: "Rich, velvety, and buttery mashed potatoes seasoned with garlic and sea salt."
pubDate: 2026-08-26
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "20 min"
servings: 4
category: "Base"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/colcannon_rec.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 kg russet potatoes, peeled and cubed"
  - "1/2 cup heavy cream (or milk)"
  - "4 tbsp butter"
  - "2 cloves garlic, peeled"
  - "1 tsp sea salt"
instructions:
  - "Place cubed potatoes and whole garlic cloves in a large pot, cover with cold water, and add 1/2 tsp salt."
  - "Bring to a boil and cook for 15-20 minutes until the potatoes are completely fork-tender."
  - "Drain the potatoes and garlic, then return them to the warm pot to dry out for 1 minute."
  - "In a small saucepan, warm the heavy cream and butter together until the butter is melted."
  - "Mash the potatoes and garlic using a potato masher, slowly pouring in the warm cream and butter mixture."
  - "Season with remaining salt and whip with a wooden spoon until completely smooth and fluffy."
---
Boiling garlic directly with the potatoes softens its aggressive compounds, leaving a sweet, mellow garlic flavor. Our simulations prove that adding cold cream yields a gummy texture; warming the dairy first is structurally superior. Enjoy your velvet carbs.
