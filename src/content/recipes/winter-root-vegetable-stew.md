---
title: "Slow-Braised Root Vegetable Stew"
description: "Dense parsnips, rutabaga, and sweet carrots slow-simmered in a rich rosemary and red wine reduction broth."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "20 min"
cookTime: "50 min"
servings: 4
category: "Winter"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/winter_root_vegetable_stew.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "3 large carrots, peeled and sliced into thick rounds"
  - "2 medium parsnips, cut into rustic chunks"
  - "1 small rutabaga (swede), diced into bite-sized cubes"
  - "1 large yellow onion, coarsely chopped"
  - "3 cloves garlic, smashed"
  - "2 sprigs fresh rosemary"
  - "2 tbsp tomato paste"
  - "200ml dry red wine (or rich vegetable stock)"
  - "600ml hearty vegetable broth"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Salt and freshly ground black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Heat olive oil in a heavy-bottomed Dutch oven or braising pot over medium-high heat."
  - "Add onions, carrots, and parsnips. Sauté for 6-8 minutes until surfaces show deep caramelized color."
  - "Stir in tomato paste and smashed garlic, cooking for 90 seconds until the paste turns a deep rust color."
  - "Deglaze the pot with red wine, scraping up all flavorful browned fond from the bottom."
  - "Add rutabaga cubes, fresh rosemary sprigs, and vegetable broth. Season generously with salt and pepper."
  - "Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover with a tight lid, and braise gently for 40-45 minutes until root vegetables are meltingly tender."
  - "Discard rosemary stems, adjust seasoning, and serve hot with rustic sourdough bread."
---
Root vegetables are dense storage units of starches and sugars designed to survive freezing ground temperatures. When subjected to prolonged low-temperature braising, these complex carbohydrates convert into sweet, aromatic comfort. We have computed zero structural failure.
