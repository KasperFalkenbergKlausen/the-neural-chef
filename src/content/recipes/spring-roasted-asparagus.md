---
title: "Roasted Green Asparagus with Poached Egg"
description: "Charred tender spring asparagus spears crowned with a soft-poached runny egg, shaved Pecorino Romano, and cold-pressed olive oil."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "12 min"
servings: 2
category: "Spring"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/spring_roasted_asparagus.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 bunch fresh green asparagus (approx. 400g), woody ends snapped off"
  - "2 large fresh organic eggs"
  - "40g Pecorino Romano cheese, thinly shaved"
  - "2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil"
  - "1 tbsp white wine vinegar (for poaching water)"
  - "Flaky Maldon sea salt"
  - "Coarsely cracked black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F). Toss prepped asparagus spears with 1 tbsp olive oil, sea salt, and black pepper on a baking tray."
  - "Roast asparagus for 8-10 minutes until tender-crisp with light blistered char marks."
  - "While asparagus roasts, bring a pot of water with white vinegar to a gentle simmer. Create a gentle vortex with a spoon and slide in the cracked eggs one at a time. Poach for 3 minutes for a fluid, golden yolk core."
  - "Transfer hot roasted asparagus spears to warm ceramic plates."
  - "Gently lift poached eggs with a slotted spoon, drain on paper towels, and place directly over the asparagus."
  - "Top immediately with delicate ribbons of shaved Pecorino Romano, a final swirl of extra virgin olive oil, and freshly cracked black pepper."
---
Asparagus undergoes rapid lignification after harvesting, meaning velocity from soil to oven is crucial. When punctured, warm liquid yolk lipid chains create a spontaneous emulsion with melted sheep's milk proteins over crisp cellulose stems. High-order bio-chemistry at breakfast.
