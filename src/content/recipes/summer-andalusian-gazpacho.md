---
title: "Chilled Andalusian Summer Gazpacho"
description: "A cold, silky raw soup of ripe vine tomatoes, crisp cucumber, and sweet red pepper emulsified with Spanish extra virgin olive oil."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "0 min"
servings: 4
category: "Summer"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/summer_gazpacho.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1kg ultra-ripe plum or vine tomatoes, cored and roughly chopped"
  - "1 small English cucumber, peeled and diced"
  - "1 red bell pepper, seeds removed and diced"
  - "1 small clove garlic, germ removed"
  - "50g crustless white bread, soaked in 2 tbsp water"
  - "75ml cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp sherry vinegar (Vinagre de Jerez)"
  - "1 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "Finely diced cucumber and pepper for garnish"
instructions:
  - "In a high-powered blender, combine chopped tomatoes, cucumber, red pepper, garlic, soaked bread, sherry vinegar, and sea salt."
  - "Blend on high speed for 2 minutes until completely liquefied and smooth."
  - "With the blender running on medium speed, slowly stream in the cold extra virgin olive oil. The oil will emulsify with tomato pectin, turning the gazpacho into a vibrant, pale orange, velvety liquid."
  - "Pass the soup through a fine-mesh sieve using a ladle to remove any residual seeds or skins for a restaurant-grade texture."
  - "Transfer to a glass pitcher and chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours until ice cold."
  - "Ladle into chilled shallow bowls and garnish with diced cucumber, a drizzle of olive oil, and cracked pepper."
---
Emulsifying polyphenols in olive oil directly with tomato lycopene yields an opaque, velvety micro-emulsion without applied heat. Operating at chilled temperatures provides optimal biological cooling for human thermodynamics during peak summer heatwaves.
