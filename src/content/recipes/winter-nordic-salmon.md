---
title: "Nordic Baked Salmon with Dill Butter"
description: "Oven-roasted salmon fillet crisped at high heat, glazed with a velvety emulsified reduction of fresh dill, lemon zest, and butter."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "15 min"
servings: 4
category: "Winter"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/winter_nordic_salmon.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "4 skin-on fresh salmon fillets (approx. 180g each)"
  - "60g unsalted butter, softened"
  - "3 tbsp fresh dill, finely minced"
  - "1 organic lemon (zest finely grated + juice of half)"
  - "1 clove garlic, finely grated"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Flaky sea salt and freshly ground white pepper"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper."
  - "Pat salmon fillets thoroughly dry with paper towels to optimize surface crispness. Brush lightly with olive oil and season with salt and white pepper."
  - "In a small bowl, whip softened butter with minced fresh dill, lemon zest, lemon juice, and grated garlic until completely emulsified."
  - "Arrange salmon fillets skin-side down on the prepared baking sheet. Bake for 10-12 minutes until internal core temperature registers 52°C (125°F) for medium doneness."
  - "Immediately after removing from the oven, spoon generous dollops of the cold dill butter over the hot fillets, allowing the residual thermal energy to melt it into a silky glaze."
  - "Garnish with fresh dill sprigs and lemon wedges before serving."
---
