featuredImage: "../../assets/images/winter_spiced_red_cabbage.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 medium head red cabbage (approx. 900g), shredded finely"
  - "2 crisp apples (such as Honeycrisp or Elstar), peeled and grated"
  - "Juice and finely grated zest of 1 large orange"
  - "3 tbsp apple cider vinegar"
  - "2 tbsp brown sugar"
  - "2 tbsp butter or duck fat"
  - "1 whole cinnamon stick"
  - "3 whole cloves"
  - "1 star anise pod"
  - "1 tsp sea salt"
instructions:
  - "Melt butter in a large heavy pot over medium heat. Add finely shredded red cabbage and sauté for 5 minutes until slightly wilted."
  - "Add grated apples, orange zest, orange juice, apple cider vinegar, and brown sugar. Toss thoroughly to coat."
  - "Nestle cinnamon stick, cloves, and star anise into the center of the cabbage."
  - "Cover with a tight lid, reduce heat to the lowest setting, and simmer gently for 40-45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender and glossy."
  - "Remove whole spices. Taste and balance with a pinch of sea salt or an extra splash of cider vinegar for bright acidity."
---
Red cabbage contains anthocyanins, natural pH-sensitive pigments that shift toward vibrant violet-red when exposed to citrus acid. By combining orange juice with gentle thermal simmering, our neural network preserves both structural crunch and visual aesthetics.
