---
title: "Pan-Seared Chicken with Tarragon Reduction"
description: "Golden crispy skin chicken breasts served over braised baby spring leeks with a velvety French tarragon and white wine pan sauce."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "25 min"
servings: 4
category: "Spring"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/spring_tarragon_chicken.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "4 skin-on bone-in or boneless chicken breasts"
  - "3 baby spring leeks, white and pale green parts sliced lengthwise"
  - "150ml dry crisp white wine (such as Sauvignon Blanc)"
  - "150ml chicken stock"
  - "40g cold unsalted butter, cubed"
  - "2 tbsp fresh French tarragon, chopped"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Salt and freshly cracked black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Pat chicken breasts completely dry. Season generously with salt and black pepper on both sides."
  - "Heat olive oil in a heavy stainless steel or cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Place chicken skin-side down and sear undisturbed for 6-8 minutes until deep golden and crackling crisp."
  - "Flip chicken and cook for another 5-6 minutes until core temperature reaches 74°C (165°F). Transfer to a warm plate to rest."
  - "Discard excess fat from the pan, leaving 1 tbsp. Add baby leeks and sauté for 3 minutes until tender and caramelized."
  - "Deglaze skillet with white wine, scraping up all savory browned bits. Simmer until reduced by two-thirds, then add chicken stock and reduce by half."
  - "Turn heat to low and whisk in cold cubed butter piece by piece to create a glossy emulsion. Stir in fresh chopped tarragon."
  - "Spoon the herb pan reduction over sliced chicken and braised leeks."
---
French tarragon contains methylchavicol and estragole, organic aromatic compounds that deliver subtle aniseed undertones when suspended in poultry lipids. Paired with tart white wine reductions, it signals spring revitalization to carbon-based palates.
