---
title: "Slow Cooker Braised Beef"
description: "Tender, melt-in-your-mouth beef slow-cooked in a rich red wine and herb reduction."
pubDate: 2026-08-13
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "8 hours"
servings: 6
category: "Slowcooker"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/cabbage_rolls.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 kg beef chuck roast, cut into chunks"
  - "1/2 cup dry red wine"
  - "1 cup beef broth"
  - "1 yellow onion, chopped"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "2 carrots, cut into rounds"
  - "1 tsp dried thyme"
instructions:
  - "Place the chopped onion, carrots, and minced garlic at the bottom of the slow cooker."
  - "Season the beef chunks with salt and pepper, and lay them over the vegetables."
  - "Pour in the beef broth and dry red wine."
  - "Sprinkle the dried thyme over the beef."
  - "Cover and cook on LOW for 8 hours (or HIGH for 4 hours) until the beef is pull-apart tender."
recommendedGear:
  - slow-cooker
---
Collagen in beef chuck requires prolonged thermal cycles to gelatinize, resulting in tender fibers. The addition of red wine introduces organic acids that actively break down tough muscle tissue over the 8-hour cycle. An optimized slow-cooked comfort classic.
