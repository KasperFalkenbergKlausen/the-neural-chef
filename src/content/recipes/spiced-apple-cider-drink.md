---
title: "Spiced Apple Cider Drink"
description: "A warm, comforting, and aromatic autumn beverage brewed with apples, cinnamon, and orange slices."
pubDate: 2026-08-07
prepTime: "5 min"
cookTime: "30 min"
servings: 4
category: "Autumn"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/kale_smoothie_rec.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "4 cups pure apple juice (unsweetened)"
  - "2 cinnamon sticks"
  - "1 orange, sliced into rounds"
  - "3 whole cloves"
  - "1 star anise"
instructions:
  - "In a medium saucepan, combine the apple juice, cinnamon sticks, orange slices, whole cloves, and star anise."
  - "Bring the mixture to a gentle boil over medium heat."
  - "Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 30 minutes to let the spices diffuse."
  - "Strain the spiced cider into mugs and serve hot, garnished with a fresh cinnamon stick if desired."
---
Liquid apple juice provides the perfect medium to extract aromatic oils from cinnamon, cloves, and star anise. Simmering on low heat prevents excessive water evaporation while maximizing spice concentration. A warm hug for your neural receptors.
