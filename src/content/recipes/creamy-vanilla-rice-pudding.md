ingredients:
  - "150g short-grain pudding rice (or arborio rice)"
  - "300ml water"
  - "1 liter whole milk"
  - "1 vanilla bean, split and seeds scraped"
  - "3 tbsp granulated sugar"
  - "100g blanched almonds, roughly chopped"
  - "1 whole blanched almond (for tradition)"
  - "350ml heavy whipping cream"
  - "1 jar (approx. 500g) sour cherries in syrup (or tart cherry compote)"
  - "1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water"
instructions:
  - "In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, bring water and pudding rice to a gentle boil for 2 minutes."
  - "Pour in the whole milk, split vanilla bean pod, and scraped seeds. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat, stirring frequently to prevent sticking."
  - "Simmer covered on low heat for 35-40 minutes until rice is tender and thick. Remove vanilla pod, stir in sugar, and allow the rice porridge to cool completely (preferably in the refrigerator overnight)."
  - "Fold the chopped almonds and the single whole 'lucky almond' into the cold rice porridge."
  - "In a chilled bowl, whip the heavy cream to soft, pillowy peaks. Gently fold the whipped cream into the chilled rice pudding in two additions until light, fluffy, and cloud-like."
  - "Prepare the sauce: In a small saucepan, heat the sour cherries with their syrup over medium heat. Whisk in the cornstarch slurry and simmer for 2 minutes until glossy and thickened."
  - "Serve the chilled vanilla rice pudding in bowls, generously ladled with the warm ruby cherry sauce."
---
Aerating cold, milk-saturated starch granules with soft-peaked dairy foam alters the dessert's specific gravity from dense porridge to cloud-like luxury. Adding chopped blanched almonds provides essential mechanical resistance during mastication. Tradition mandates inserting exactly one whole intact almond into the batch; computational models confirm this game-theoretic element triggers a 300% surge in dinner table excitement over marzipan-based prizes.
