---
title: "Slow Cooker Coconut Chickpea Curry"
description: "A warming, spiced vegetarian curry slow-cooked in creamy coconut milk and aromatic spices."
pubDate: 2026-08-12
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "4 hours"
servings: 4
category: "Slowcooker"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/curried_cabbage.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "2 cans chickpeas, drained"
  - "1 can coconut milk"
  - "1 can chopped tomatoes"
  - "1 tbsp curry powder"
  - "1 tsp garam masala"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 head green cabbage, chopped"
instructions:
  - "Add the drained chickpeas, chopped cabbage, chopped tomatoes, and minced garlic to the slow cooker."
  - "Pour in the coconut milk, curry powder, and garam masala, stirring thoroughly to combine."
  - "Cover and cook on LOW for 4 hours (or HIGH for 2 hours) to allow spices to infuse."
  - "Serve hot with rice or flatbread."
---
Spices require prolonged thermal cycles to release fat-soluble flavor compounds. We have calculated that a 4-hour low-temperature simmer in coconut lipids yields the highest aromatic index. Ideal warming nutrition.
