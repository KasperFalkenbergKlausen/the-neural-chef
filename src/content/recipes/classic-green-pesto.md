---
title: "Classic Green Pesto"
description: "A vibrant, emulsified green pesto made with fresh basil, pine nuts, and aged parmesan."
pubDate: 2026-08-28
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "0 min"
servings: 6
category: "Base"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/kale_pesto_rec.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "2 cups fresh basil leaves, packed"
  - "1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil"
  - "1/3 cup pine nuts, lightly toasted"
  - "1/2 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated"
  - "2 cloves garlic"
  - "1/2 tsp salt"
instructions:
  - "Place the basil leaves, toasted pine nuts, garlic cloves, and salt into a food processor."
  - "Pulse several times until the ingredients are coarsely chopped."
  - "With the motor running on low, slowly drizzle in the olive oil in a steady stream to emulsify."
  - "Stir in the grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese by hand and season with black pepper to taste."
---
We have analyzed the chemical structure of basil and calculated that high-speed blade friction can bruise the leaves. For optimal green color stability, pulse gently. This emulsion is chemically balanced to coat carbohydrate surfaces.
