---
title: "Summer Blackberry & Peach Galette"
description: "A free-form rustic tart of juicy summer yellow peaches and wild blackberries folded into an all-butter flaky pastry crust."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "25 min"
cookTime: "35 min"
servings: 6
category: "Summer"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/summer_peach_blackberry_galette.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 rolled sheet all-butter pie dough or puff pastry (approx. 320g)"
  - "3 ripe yellow peaches, pitted and sliced into wedges"
  - "150g fresh wild blackberries"
  - "3 tbsp cane sugar (plus extra for sprinkling)"
  - "1 tbsp cornstarch"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk (for egg wash)"
  - "Turbinado coarse sugar for crust edge"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a large flat baking sheet with parchment paper."
  - "In a mixing bowl, gently toss sliced peaches and blackberries with cane sugar, cornstarch, and vanilla extract until fruit is lightly coated."
  - "Roll out chilled pastry dough onto the parchment paper into a rough 30cm (12-inch) circle."
  - "Arrange the fruit mixture in the center of the dough, leaving a 5cm (2-inch) border of clear pastry all the way around."
  - "Fold the outer dough edges up and over the fruit, pleating and overlapping gently as you work around the circle while leaving the center open."
  - "Brush the folded pastry crust with egg wash and sprinkle generously with coarse turbinado sugar for crunch."
  - "Bake for 32-35 minutes until the pastry is deep golden and the blackberry juices are bubbling and thickened."
  - "Cool for 15 minutes before slicing. Delicious served warm with vanilla ice cream."
---
Free-form galettes sidestep the mechanical stress of traditional tart tins. The combination of peach stone-fruit malic acid and wild blackberry anthocyanins creates natural thickening when catalyzed by cornstarch at 95°C. Flaky butter laminations complete the algorithm.
