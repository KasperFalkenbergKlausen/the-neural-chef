title: "Shaved Fennel, Radish & Citrus Salad"
description: "Paper-thin ribbons of crisp Florence fennel bulb and spicy red radishes dressed in orange blossom citrus vinaigrette with fresh dill."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "0 min"
servings: 4
category: "Spring"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/spring_fennel_salad.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "2 crisp fennel bulbs, trimmed and shaved paper-thin (reserve green fronds)"
  - "1 bunch fresh red radishes (approx. 8-10 radishes), thinly mandoline-sliced"
  - "Juice of 1 fresh blood orange or navel orange"
  - "1 tbsp white balsamic vinegar"
  - "3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil"
  - "1/2 tsp orange blossom water (optional but aromatic)"
  - "2 tbsp fresh dill sprigs"
  - "Flaky sea salt and pink peppercorns"
instructions:
  - "Using a mandoline slicer, shave trimmed fennel bulbs and red radishes into paper-thin translucent ribbons."
  - "Submerge shaved vegetables in an ice water bath for 5 minutes to maximize cellular turgor pressure (extra crisp crunch), then spin thoroughly dry in a salad spinner."
  - "In a small glass bowl, whisk fresh orange juice, white balsamic vinegar, orange blossom water, and olive oil with a pinch of flaky salt."
  - "Arrange chilled fennel ribbons and crimson radish slices in a wide serving dish."
  - "Drizzle evenly with citrus dressing. Scatter reserved delicate fennel fronds, fresh dill sprigs, and crushed pink peppercorns over the top before serving."
---
Plant turgor pressure decreases after harvest as cell moisture evaporates. By shock-soaking thin vegetable ribbons in ice-cold water, cells rehydrate through passive osmosis, returning structural crispness to factory specifications.
