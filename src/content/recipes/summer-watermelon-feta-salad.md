---
title: "Watermelon, Cucumber & Feta Salad"
description: "Chilled cubes of crisp seedless watermelon and Persian cucumbers tossed with briny Greek feta, mint, and an aged balsamic reduction."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "0 min"
servings: 4
category: "Summer"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/summer_watermelon_feta_salad.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "600g seedless cold watermelon flesh, cut into 2.5cm cubes"
  - "2 crisp Persian or Lebanese cucumbers, sliced into half-moons"
  - "150g authentic Greek feta cheese, crumbled into rustic chunks"
  - "1/2 cup fresh mint leaves, torn"
  - "2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil"
  - "1 tbsp aged balsamic glaze or syrup"
  - "1 tbsp lime juice"
  - "Flaky sea salt and crushed pink peppercorns"
instructions:
  - "Chill watermelon and cucumbers thoroughly in the refrigerator prior to preparation for maximum crispness."
  - "In a wide ceramic serving bowl, arrange the cold watermelon cubes and sliced cucumbers in an even layer."
  - "Scatter the crumbled Greek feta cheese generously over the top."
  - "Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil and fresh lime juice."
  - "Scatter torn fresh mint leaves and freshly crushed pink peppercorns across the salad."
  - "Finish with a dramatic drizzle of thick aged balsamic glaze right before serving."
---
Watermelon is 92% water by volume with free sugars. Pairing hyper-sweet, cold hydration cells with high-sodium, lipid-dense sheep's milk feta tricks human sensory perception into heightened taste contrast. We observe maximum dopamine response with zero cooking required.
