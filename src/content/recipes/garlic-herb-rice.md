---
title: "Garlic Herb Infused Rice"
description: "Perfectly fluffy long-grain rice infused with garlic essence and fresh garden herbs."
pubDate: 2026-08-23
prepTime: "5 min"
cookTime: "20 min"
servings: 4
category: "Base"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/cabbage_rice_rec.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1.5 cups long-grain white rice"
  - "3 cups vegetable stock"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped"
  - "1 tsp salt"
instructions:
  - "Rinse the rice under cold water until the water runs clear to remove excess starch."
  - "In a medium pot, heat olive oil over medium heat and sauté the minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Add the rinsed rice and toast it in the oil and garlic for 2 minutes, stirring constantly."
  - "Pour in the vegetable stock and salt, bringing the mixture to a boil."
  - "Reduce heat to low, cover with a tight lid, and simmer for 15 minutes without opening the lid."
  - "Remove from heat and let sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and stir in the fresh parsley."
