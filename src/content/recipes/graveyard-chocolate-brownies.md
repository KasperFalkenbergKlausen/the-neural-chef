ingredients:
  - "200g dark chocolate (70% cocoa solids), chopped"
  - "150g unsalted butter, cubed"
  - "200g granulated sugar"
  - "3 large eggs, at room temperature"
  - "75g all-purpose flour"
  - "40g Dutch-processed cocoa powder"
  - "1/2 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "10 chocolate sandwich cookies (like Oreos), crushed into fine soil"
  - "9 rectangular shortbread cookies or Milano cookies (for tombstones)"
  - "50g melted dark chocolate in a piping bag (to pipe RIP text)"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F) and line a 20x20 cm (8x8 inch) square baking pan with parchment paper."
  - "Melt the chopped dark chocolate and cubed butter together gently in a heatproof bowl set over simmering water (or in short microwave bursts). Stir until glossy and smooth, then let cool for 5 minutes."
  - "Whisk eggs and sugar together in a separate bowl for 3 minutes until pale and slightly frothy, then slowly fold in the melted chocolate mixture."
  - "Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and sea salt. Fold gently with a spatula just until no dry flour streaks remain—do not overmix."
  - "Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for 25-28 minutes until the edges are set and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with moist, fudgy crumbs."
  - "While warm, pipe 'R.I.P.' onto each shortbread cookie with melted chocolate. Let set."
  - "Once the brownie is slightly cooled, scatter the crushed chocolate cookie soil over the top. Cut small slits with a paring knife and stand each tombstone upright into each brownie square."
