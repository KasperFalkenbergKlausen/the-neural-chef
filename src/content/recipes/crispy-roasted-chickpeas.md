---
title: "Crispy Roasted Chickpeas"
description: "A simple, crunchy, and spiced snack made by roasting chickpeas until perfectly crisp."
pubDate: 2026-08-25
prepTime: "5 min"
cookTime: "25 min"
servings: 4
category: "Base"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/crispy_roasted_chickpeas.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "2 cans chickpeas, rinsed and dried completely"
  - "1.5 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "1/2 tsp garlic powder"
  - "1/2 tsp sea salt"
instructions:
  - "Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F)."
  - "Spread the rinsed chickpeas on a clean kitchen towel and roll them around until they are 100% dry."
  - "Toss the dried chickpeas with olive oil and sea salt in a bowl (save the spices for later to prevent burning)."
  - "Spread the chickpeas in a single layer on a baking sheet."
  - "Roast for 20-25 minutes, shaking the pan halfway through, until they are crispy and golden."
  - "Remove from the oven, immediately toss with the smoked paprika and garlic powder while hot, and let cool completely to crisp up."
recommendedGear:
  - sheet-pan
---
Moisture inside a chickpea skin prevents crisping by trapping steam. We have calculated that drying them thoroughly and adding spices after roasting preserves flavor oils without carbonizing them. An optimized, crunchy protein bite.
