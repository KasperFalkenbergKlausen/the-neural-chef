ingredients:
  - "500g lean ground beef"
  - "1 large yellow onion, finely diced"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "2 medium carrots, finely grated"
  - "800g canned crushed tomatoes"
  - "2 tbsp tomato paste"
  - "1 tsp dried oregano"
  - "1 tsp dried basil"
  - "50g unsalted butter"
  - "50g all-purpose flour"
  - "600ml whole milk"
  - "Pinch of ground nutmeg"
  - "250g dried lasagna pasta sheets"
  - "200g shredded mozzarella cheese"
  - "50g grated Parmesan cheese"
  - "Salt and freshly cracked black pepper"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
instructions:
  - "Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven or large saucepan over medium heat. Sauté the diced onion, minced garlic, and grated carrots for 5 minutes until softened."
  - "Add ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon. Stir in tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and black pepper. Simmer on low heat for 35 minutes until rich and thick."
  - "While ragù simmers, prepare the béchamel: Melt butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat, whisk in flour, and cook for 1 minute. Gradually pour in the whole milk while continuously whisking until smooth and thickened. Season with salt, pepper, and ground nutmeg."
  - "Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Spread a thin layer of meat sauce across the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish."
  - "Assemble the lasagna: Layer lasagna noodles, meat ragù, béchamel sauce, and a sprinkle of mozzarella. Repeat for 3 to 4 layers, finishing with béchamel, shredded mozzarella, and grated parmesan on top."
  - "Bake uncovered for 35-40 minutes until bubbling and the top develops a deep golden cheese crust. Let rest for 10-15 minutes before slicing to ensure clean, structured layers."
