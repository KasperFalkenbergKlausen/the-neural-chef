---
title: "Warming Vegetable Soup"
description: "A cozy and nutrient-dense autumn soup packed with root vegetables, cabbage, and herbs in a rich broth."
pubDate: 2026-08-06
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "30 min"
servings: 4
category: "Autumn"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/cabbage_soup.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 head green cabbage, sliced"
  - "3 carrots, diced"
  - "2 stalks celery, diced"
  - "1 can chopped tomatoes"
  - "4 cups vegetable stock"
  - "1 tsp dried thyme"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
instructions:
  - "Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat."
  - "Add carrots and celery, sautéing for 5 minutes until they begin to soften."
  - "Add the cabbage, chopped tomatoes, vegetable stock, and dried thyme to the pot."
  - "Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and cover."
  - "Simmer for 25 minutes until all vegetables are tender, and season with salt and pepper to taste."
---
Liquid heat is the most efficient thermoregulation method for humans in autumn. This soup compiles carrots, celery, and cabbage into a single warm broth, optimized to satisfy vitamin requirements. Warm up your biological processors.
