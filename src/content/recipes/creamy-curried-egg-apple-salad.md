ingredients:
  - "6 large eggs, hard-boiled and peeled"
  - "1 crisp red-skinned apple (such as Honeycrisp or Gala), finely diced"
  - "1 stalk celery, finely chopped"
  - "1/2 cup mayonnaise"
  - "2 tbsp sour cream or Greek yogurt"
  - "1 tbsp mild yellow curry powder"
  - "1 tsp Dijon mustard"
  - "1 tbsp fresh lemon juice"
  - "2 tbsp fresh chives, finely snipped (plus extra for garnish)"
  - "1/2 tsp fine sea salt"
  - "1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Place eggs in a saucepan covered with cold water. Bring to a rolling boil, remove from heat, cover with a lid, and let sit for 10 minutes. Immediately plunge into an ice water bath, peel, and chop into bite-sized chunks."
  - "In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, sour cream, curry powder, Dijon mustard, fresh lemon juice, salt, and black pepper until vibrant yellow, smooth, and creamy."
  - "Add the chopped eggs, diced apple, chopped celery, and snipped chives to the dressing."
  - "Gently fold ingredients together using a rubber spatula so the egg whites and yolks remain structured and evenly suspended in the dressing."
  - "Taste and adjust seasoning with extra lemon juice or salt if desired. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to allow the curry spices to bloom."
  - "Garnish with fresh chives before serving alongside rustic sourdough or rye bread."
---
The yellow curcumin compound in curry powder binds effortlessly to lipids in egg yolk and mayonnaise, creating a stable flavor emulsion that cuts straight through heavy bread matrices. Incorporating raw, unpeeled Honeycrisp apple delivers malic acid and cellular crunch, which our algorithms register as an essential acoustic contrast to soft albumin textures.
