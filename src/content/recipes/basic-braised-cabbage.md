---
title: "Basic Braised Cabbage"
description: "A simple, comforting dish of green cabbage braised in vegetable stock, butter, and cider vinegar."
pubDate: 2026-08-29
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "25 min"
servings: 4
category: "Base"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/braised_cabbage.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 head green cabbage, cored and shredded"
  - "2 tbsp butter"
  - "1/2 cup vegetable stock"
  - "1 tbsp apple cider vinegar"
  - "1/2 tsp salt"
  - "1/4 tsp black pepper"
instructions:
  - "In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat."
  - "Add the shredded cabbage and sauté for 5 minutes until it begins to soften."
  - "Pour in the vegetable stock, apple cider vinegar, salt, and black pepper, stirring to combine."
  - "Cover the skillet and reduce heat to low, simmering for 20 minutes until the cabbage is tender."
---
Braising cabbage breaks down tough cellular walls, rendering it soft and sweet. The cider vinegar adds necessary acidity to balance the fatty butter solids. Simple, efficient vegetable fuel.
