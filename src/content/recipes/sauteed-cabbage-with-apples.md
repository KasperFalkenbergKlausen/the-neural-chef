---
title: "Sauteed Cabbage with Apples"
description: "A quick, sweet, and savory skillet sauté of shredded green cabbage, sweet apples, and cider vinegar."
pubDate: 2026-08-18
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "15 min"
servings: 4
category: "Autumn"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/sesame_slaw.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1/2 head green cabbage, shredded"
  - "1 sweet apple, cored and thinly sliced"
  - "1 small red onion, sliced"
  - "1.5 tbsp butter"
  - "1 tbsp apple cider vinegar"
  - "1/2 tsp salt"
instructions:
  - "In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium-high heat."
  - "Add the sliced onion and apple, sautéing for 4 minutes until they begin to soften and brown."
  - "Add the shredded cabbage and salt, stirring to combine."
  - "Sauté for 8-10 minutes, stirring frequently, until the cabbage is tender-crisp."
  - "Drizzle with apple cider vinegar in the final minute of cooking, toss well, and serve hot."
---
Apples soften and release fructose when heated, which coats the bitter green cabbage fibers in a sweet glaze. Sautéing quickly preserves a pleasant crunch while caramelizing the onion sugars. A balanced autumn skillet.
