---
title: "Grilled Honey-Lime Chicken Skewers"
description: "Char-grilled skewers of tender marinated chicken breast and shaved summer zucchini ribbons with a sticky chili-honey glaze."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "20 min"
cookTime: "12 min"
servings: 4
category: "Summer"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/summer_grilled_chicken_skewers.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "650g chicken breast or thigh fillets, cut into 3cm bite-sized chunks"
  - "2 medium green zucchinis, sliced into thin ribbons with a peeler"
  - "3 tbsp wildflower honey"
  - "Juice and zest of 2 fresh limes"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 cloves garlic, finely minced"
  - "1/2 tsp chili flakes"
  - "Fresh cilantro leaves for garnish"
  - "Bamboo or metal skewers"
instructions:
  - "If using bamboo skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes to prevent scorching on the grill."
  - "In a medium bowl, whisk honey, lime juice, lime zest, olive oil, minced garlic, chili flakes, 1 tsp salt, and pepper."
  - "Add chicken cubes to the marinade, tossing to coat. Rest for 15-20 minutes."
  - "Thread skewers by alternating chunks of marinated chicken with accordion-folded ribbons of fresh zucchini."
  - "Preheat grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. Lightly oil the grill grates."
  - "Grill skewers for 10-12 minutes, turning every 3 minutes and brushing with remaining marinade, until chicken is cooked through (internal temp 74°C / 165°F) with caramelized edges."
  - "Transfer to a serving platter, scatter fresh cilantro leaves, and serve with lime wedges."
---
The fructose content in honey triggers rapid caramelization at 160°C, producing attractive blistered char marks without drying out poultry breast tissue. Zucchini ribbons provide hydration buffering between chicken chunks. System evaluation: highly palatable.
