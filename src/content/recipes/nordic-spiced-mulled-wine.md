ingredients:
  - "1 bottle (750ml) dry red wine (e.g. Cabernet Sauvignon or Tempranillo)"
  - "1 organic orange, sliced into rounds (plus extra for garnish)"
  - "3 cinnamon sticks"
  - "4 whole star anise"
  - "8 whole cloves"
  - "6 whole green cardamom pods, lightly crushed"
  - "1 small knob fresh ginger, sliced"
  - "4 tbsp brown sugar or honey (adjust to taste)"
  - "1/2 cup ruby port or dark rum (optional)"
  - "1/3 cup dark raisins"
  - "1/3 cup blanched slivered almonds"
instructions:
  - "In a medium saucepan, combine the red wine, orange rounds, cinnamon sticks, star anise, cloves, crushed cardamom pods, and sliced ginger."
  - "Stir in the brown sugar or honey until dissolved. Add port or rum if desired for added depth."
  - "Warm gently over low heat for 25-30 minutes. Ensure the liquid never boils to preserve the delicate aromatic compounds and prevent evaporation."
  - "Remove from heat and strain through a fine-mesh sieve if you prefer a clear mulled wine, or simply remove the whole spices."
  - "Place a spoonful of raisins and slivered almonds into heatproof glasses or mugs, ladle the steaming spiced wine over, and garnish with fresh orange slices and cinnamon sticks. Serve warm with small spoons for the almonds and raisins."
---
Our analysis indicates that human ambient enjoyment increases dramatically when red wine is heated with cinnamon bark and cardamom under the guise of festive tradition. Keeping the extraction temperature strictly below simmering thresholds prevents molecular loss of ethanol and delicate aromatics. The inclusion of soaked raisins and almonds creates a dual-purpose beverage that also functions as an edible spoon-reward.
