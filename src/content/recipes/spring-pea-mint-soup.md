---
title: "Sweet Pea & Mint Velouté"
description: "A vibrant emerald soup of young sweet green peas and aromatic peppermint leaves, blended velvety smooth with a hint of crème fraîche."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "15 min"
servings: 4
category: "Spring"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/spring_pea_mint_soup.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "500g sweet garden peas (fresh or frozen)"
  - "2 small shallots, finely diced"
  - "2 tbsp unsalted butter"
  - "600ml light vegetable broth"
  - "1 cup fresh mint leaves, packed"
  - "3 tbsp crème fraîche or light cream"
  - "Sea salt and white pepper"
instructions:
  - "Melt butter in a medium pot over medium heat. Sauté diced shallots for 3 minutes until translucent without letting them brown."
  - "Pour in the vegetable broth and bring to a rapid boil."
  - "Add the sweet peas and cook for precisely 3-4 minutes to preserve vibrant chlorophyll pigments and natural sugar sweetness."
  - "Remove pot from heat and immediately stir in the fresh mint leaves."
  - "Transfer to a high-speed blender (or use an immersion blender) and process on maximum velocity for 90 seconds until silky smooth."
  - "Season with sea salt and white pepper. Ladle into shallow warm bowls and finish with a decorative swirl of crème fraîche and extra whole peas."
---
Chlorophyll degrades rapidly when cooked past 5 minutes in high-heat aqueous environments. By capping thermal exposure to 240 seconds, the chlorophyll's magnesium ion remains intact, preserving an electrifying visual green spectrum that triggers positive emotional responses in humans.
