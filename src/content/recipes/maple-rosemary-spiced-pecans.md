ingredients:
  - "350g raw pecan halves"
  - "3 tbsp pure maple syrup"
  - "1 tbsp melted unsalted butter (or coconut oil)"
  - "2 tbsp fresh rosemary, finely minced"
  - "1 tsp flaky sea salt (plus extra for finishing)"
  - "1/2 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for subtle heat)"
  - "1 tbsp brown sugar"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F) and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper."
  - "In a medium bowl, whisk together the melted butter, maple syrup, brown sugar, minced fresh rosemary, flaky sea salt, smoked paprika, and cayenne pepper until emulsified."
  - "Add the raw pecan halves to the bowl and toss thoroughly until every pecan is evenly coated in the aromatic glaze."
  - "Spread the pecans in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet, ensuring they do not overlap."
  - "Roast for 12-15 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until fragrant, deeply golden, and sizzling."
  - "Remove from the oven, sprinkle immediately with a light pinch of extra flaky sea salt, and allow to cool completely on the baking sheet. As the maple glaze cools, the coating turns crisp and crunchy."
