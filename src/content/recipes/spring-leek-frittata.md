---
title: "Spring Leek & Goat Cheese Frittata"
description: "Fluffy pasture-raised baked eggs folded with tender caramelized spring leeks, creamy fresh goat cheese curds, and snips of garden chives."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "20 min"
servings: 4
category: "Spring"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/spring_leek_frittata.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "8 large pasture-raised eggs"
  - "2 medium spring leeks, thinly sliced (white and light green parts)"
  - "100g fresh creamy goat cheese (chèvre), crumbled"
  - "60ml whole milk or cream"
  - "2 tbsp unsalted butter"
  - "2 tbsp fresh garden chives, finely snipped"
  - "1/2 tsp sea salt"
  - "Freshly ground black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). In a mixing bowl, whisk eggs with milk, salt, black pepper, and half the snipped chives until airy and completely homogenized."
  - "Melt butter in a 25cm (10-inch) oven-safe cast iron skillet over medium-low heat. Add sliced leeks and sauté gently for 7-8 minutes until soft, sweet, and lightly golden."
  - "Pour whisked egg mixture evenly over the caramelized leeks. Cook on the stovetop undisturbed for 2 minutes until bottom and edges begin to set."
  - "Dot the top surface evenly with soft crumbled goat cheese."
  - "Transfer the skillet into the preheated oven and bake for 12-14 minutes until center is puffed and barely set."
  - "Cool for 5 minutes in skillet, scatter remaining fresh chives across the top, slice into generous wedges, and serve."
---
Eggs set into rigid protein lattices at precisely 65°C to 70°C. Introducing moisture via milk and pockets of tart goat milk fat disrupts aggressive protein coagulation, ensuring a tender custard rather than an accidental rubber disc. We recommend prompt consumption.
