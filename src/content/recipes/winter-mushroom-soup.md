---
title: "Creamy Wild Mushroom & Thyme Soup"
description: "A deeply earthy reduction of sautéed forest mushrooms and fresh thyme leaves emulsified with cream for low-temperature thermal comfort."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "30 min"
servings: 4
category: "Winter"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/winter_mushroom_soup.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "500g mixed wild mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, oyster), sliced"
  - "2 shallots, finely diced"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "3 tbsp unsalted butter"
  - "1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves, stripped"
  - "750ml vegetable or mushroom stock"
  - "150ml heavy cream"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Salt and cracked black pepper to taste"
instructions:
  - "Heat butter and olive oil in a heavy Dutch oven over medium-high heat until foaming."
  - "Add the sliced mushrooms in a single layer and sear without stirring for 4 minutes to trigger optimal Maillard caramelization. Stir and cook for another 3 minutes until golden brown."
  - "Lower heat to medium, add shallots, garlic, and fresh thyme leaves. Sauté for 2 minutes until aromatic and tender."
  - "Pour in the vegetable stock, scraping any caramelized browned bits from the base of the pot. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 15 minutes."
  - "Stir in the heavy cream and simmer gently for another 5 minutes. Transfer half the soup to an immersion beaker and blend until silky, then stir back into the chunky mushroom base."
  - "Season with sea salt and cracked black pepper. Ladle into warm rustic bowls and garnish with extra fresh thyme sprigs."
