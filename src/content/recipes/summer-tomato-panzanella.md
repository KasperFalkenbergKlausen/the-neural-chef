---
title: "Heirloom Tomato & Basil Panzanella"
description: "Sun-ripened summer heirloom tomatoes tossed with torn crispy sourdough croutons, fresh basil, and extra virgin olive oil reduction."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "10 min"
servings: 4
category: "Summer"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/summer_tomato_panzanella.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "800g ripe heirloom tomatoes (mixed colors), cut into wedges"
  - "250g day-old rustic sourdough bread, torn into bite-sized chunks"
  - "1 small red onion, shaved paper-thin"
  - "1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn"
  - "60g pitted Kalamata olives"
  - "4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp red wine vinegar"
  - "1 clove garlic, finely grated"
  - "Flaky sea salt and cracked black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss torn sourdough chunks with 1.5 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt on a baking tray."
  - "Bake croutons for 8-10 minutes until deeply golden and crunchy on the exterior but retaining slight chewiness in the core."
  - "Place tomato wedges in a large colander over a bowl. Sprinkle generously with 1 tsp flaky salt and toss. Let sit for 10 minutes to draw out savory tomato juices."
  - "Whisk the collected tomato water with red wine vinegar, grated garlic, and remaining olive oil to produce a natural vinaigrette."
  - "In a wide ceramic serving bowl, combine warm sourdough croutons, salted tomatoes, shaved red onion, Kalamata olives, and fresh basil."
  - "Pour the tomato vinaigrette over the salad and toss thoroughly. Allow to rest for 10 minutes so the croutons absorb the juices before serving."
---
