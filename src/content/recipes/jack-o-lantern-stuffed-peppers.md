ingredients:
  - "4 large orange bell peppers, washed with tops sliced off and seeds removed"
  - "450g lean ground beef (or ground turkey)"
  - "1.5 cups cooked long-grain rice"
  - "1 small yellow onion, finely diced"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 cup crushed canned tomatoes"
  - "1 tsp smoked paprika"
  - "1/2 tsp ground cumin"
  - "1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese, divided"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Fine sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Using a small paring knife, carefully carve triangle eyes, a nose, and a zig-zag smile onto one side of each hollowed orange pepper."
  - "Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil and par-boil the carved peppers for 3 minutes to tenderize. Drain and place cut-side up in a baking dish."
  - "Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté diced onions and minced garlic for 3 minutes, then add ground beef and brown completely."
  - "Stir in crushed tomatoes, smoked paprika, cumin, cooked rice, half of the cheddar cheese, salt, and pepper. Cook for 2 minutes until evenly combined."
  - "Spoon the beef-and-rice mixture generously into each carved pepper. Top with remaining cheddar cheese."
  - "Bake at 190°C (375°F) on a rimmed baking sheet for 25-30 minutes until peppers are tender and the cheese topping is bubbly and golden."
