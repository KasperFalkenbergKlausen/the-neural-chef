---
title: "Apple Cinnamon Slaw"
description: "A fresh and crunchy autumn slaw combining shredded cabbage, sweet apples, and a touch of warm cinnamon."
pubDate: 2026-08-30
prepTime: "15 min"
cookTime: "0 min"
servings: 4
category: "Autumn"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/apple_slaw_rec.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1/2 head red cabbage, finely shredded"
  - "2 sweet apples (like Honeycrisp), sliced into matchsticks"
  - "1/4 cup greek yogurt"
  - "1 tbsp honey"
  - "1 tsp apple cider vinegar"
  - "1/4 tsp ground cinnamon"
instructions:
  - "In a large bowl, combine the finely shredded red cabbage and the sweet apple matchsticks."
  - "In a separate small bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, honey, apple cider vinegar, and ground cinnamon."
  - "Pour the yogurt dressing over the cabbage and apple mixture."
  - "Toss thoroughly until everything is evenly coated, then cover and chill for 10 minutes before serving."
