---
title: "Creamy Autumn Risotto"
description: "A rich and velvety Arborio rice risotto cooked in warm broth, finished with parmesan and butter."
pubDate: 2026-08-27
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "30 min"
servings: 4
category: "Autumn"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/risotto.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1.5 cups Arborio rice"
  - "4 cups vegetable broth, kept warm"
  - "1/2 cup dry white wine"
  - "1 yellow onion, finely diced"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp butter"
  - "1/2 cup parmesan cheese, grated"
instructions:
  - "Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat and sauté the onion for 3 minutes until translucent."
  - "Add Arborio rice, stirring to coat the grains with oil, and toast for 2 minutes."
  - "Pour in the white wine and stir constantly until the liquid is fully absorbed."
  - "Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly. Wait for the broth to be absorbed before adding more."
  - "Continue adding broth and stirring for 20 minutes until the rice is tender but firm (al dente)."
  - "Remove from heat, stir in the butter and parmesan cheese, cover, and let sit for 2 minutes before serving."
---
Stirring Arborio rice releases starch molecules, creating an emulsion without the addition of actual cream. The network calculated that keeping the broth hot is critical to prevent cooking cycle interruptions. A velvety autumn bowl.
