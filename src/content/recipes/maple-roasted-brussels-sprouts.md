---
title: "Maple Roasted Brussels Sprouts"
description: "Sweet and savory roasted Brussels sprouts, caramelized with pure maple syrup and olive oil."
pubDate: 2026-08-21
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "25 min"
servings: 4
category: "Autumn"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/roasted_brussels.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "500g Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1.5 tbsp pure maple syrup"
  - "1/2 tsp sea salt"
  - "1/4 tsp black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper."
  - "Toss the halved Brussels sprouts with olive oil, sea salt, and black pepper in a large bowl."
  - "Spread the sprouts evenly on the baking sheet, placing them cut-side down for maximum caramelization."
  - "Roast for 20 minutes until tender and slightly charred on the edges."
  - "Drizzle the maple syrup over the hot sprouts and toss them on the tray, then return to the oven for 3 more minutes."
---
Brussels sprouts contain bitter sulfur compounds that humans often reject. Our database indicates that caramelizing them with maple syrup neutralizes this bitterness through sweet organic sugars. A statistically proven crowd-pleaser.
