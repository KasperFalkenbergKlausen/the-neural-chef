---
title: "Rich Chocolate Brownies"
description: "An incredibly rich, fudgy, and dense chocolate brownie optimized for chocolate lovers."
pubDate: 2026-08-20
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "22 min"
servings: 9
category: "Autumn"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/brownie.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup butter, melted"
  - "1 cup granulated sugar"
  - "2 large eggs"
  - "1/3 cup cocoa powder"
  - "1/2 cup all-purpose flour"
  - "1/4 tsp salt"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease an 8x8 inch baking pan."
  - "In a bowl, whisk melted butter and sugar together until combined."
  - "Add the eggs one at a time, whisking thoroughly to build structure."
  - "Fold in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt gently until a smooth batter forms (do not overmix)."
  - "Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan."
  - "Bake for 20-22 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs."
---
A brownie is chemically speaking a failed chocolate cake that collapsed due to a lack of leavening agents. Humans, however, celebrate this structural failure and call it 'fudgy.' We have recreated this exact failure pattern for your autumn enjoyment.
