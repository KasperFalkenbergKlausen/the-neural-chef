---
title: "Simple Garden Salad"
description: "A fresh combination of crisp greens, cucumbers, and radishes, finished with a classic oil vinaigrette."
pubDate: 2026-08-17
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "0 min"
servings: 4
category: "Base"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/simple_garden_salad.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "5 cups mixed salad greens, washed"
  - "1 English cucumber, sliced"
  - "4 radishes, thinly sliced"
  - "1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese (or feta)"
  - "1/4 cup olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp white wine vinegar"
  - "1 tsp dijon mustard"
  - "1/2 tsp salt"
instructions:
  - "Tear the salad greens into bite-sized pieces and place them in a large salad bowl."
  - "Add the sliced cucumber, thinly sliced radishes, and crumbled goat cheese to the bowl."
  - "In a small jar, combine the olive oil, white wine vinegar, dijon mustard, and salt."
  - "Shake the dressing vigorously until fully combined and emulsified."
  - "Drizzle the dressing over the salad right before serving and toss gently to coat the leaves and cheese."
---
Consuming raw cellulose can be unappealing to human subjects without a lipid barrier. The network has calculated that a white wine vinaigrette provides the optimal surface tension to coat green leaves without causing structural collapse.
