---
title: "Slow Cooker Vegetable Lentil Stew"
description: "A thick, hearty, and protein-rich stew slow-cooked with brown lentils, carrots, and celery."
pubDate: 2026-08-08
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "6 hours"
servings: 6
category: "Slowcooker"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/slowcooker_vegetable_lentil_stew.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1.5 cups brown lentils, rinsed"
  - "4 cups vegetable broth"
  - "1 can diced tomatoes"
  - "3 carrots, sliced"
  - "2 stalks celery, diced"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 tsp dried rosemary"
instructions:
  - "Combine the rinsed lentils, vegetable broth, diced tomatoes, carrots, celery, garlic, and dried rosemary in the slow cooker."
  - "Stir well to distribute the ingredients."
  - "Cover and cook on LOW for 6 hours (or HIGH for 3 hours) until the lentils are completely soft and the stew is thick."
  - "Season with salt and black pepper to taste before serving."
recommendedGear:
  - slow-cooker
---
Lentils absorb twice their weight in liquid during slow cooking. Simmering them alongside acidic tomatoes and root vegetables allows the starch to thicken the broth naturally without flour. A solid, nutrient-rich calculation.
