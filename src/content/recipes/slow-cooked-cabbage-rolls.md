title: "Slow Cooked Cabbage Rolls"
description: "Seasoned ground beef and rice wrapped in cabbage leaves, slow-cooked in a rich tomato sauce."
pubDate: 2026-08-16
prepTime: "30 min"
cookTime: "6 hours"
servings: 6
category: "Slowcooker"
tags:
  - "Autumn"
  - "Comfort Food"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/cabbage_rolls.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "1 head Savoy cabbage"
  - "500g ground beef"
  - "1 cup cooked rice"
  - "1 yellow onion, finely diced"
  - "400g tomato sauce"
  - "2 tbsp brown sugar"
  - "1 tbsp apple cider vinegar"
instructions:
  - "Blanch cabbage leaves in boiling water for 3 minutes until tender and let cool."
  - "In a bowl, mix ground beef, cooked rice, onion, and 1/4 cup of the tomato sauce. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Place a portion of beef mixture in each cabbage leaf, roll tightly, and tuck in the sides."
  - "Choose your preferred preparation protocol below:"
  - "SLOW COOKER METHOD: Arrange the cabbage rolls seam-side down in the slow cooker pot. Whisk the remaining tomato sauce, brown sugar, and vinegar together, pour over the rolls, cover, and cook on LOW for 6 hours (or HIGH for 3 hours)."
  - "OVEN CASSEROLE METHOD: Arrange the cabbage rolls seam-side down in a greased ceramic baking dish or Romertopf. Whisk the remaining tomato sauce, brown sugar, and vinegar together, pour over the rolls, cover with a lid or foil, and bake at 180°C (350°F) for 50 minutes. Remove cover and bake for 10 minutes more until bubbling."
recommendedGear:
