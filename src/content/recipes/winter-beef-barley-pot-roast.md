---
title: "Slow-Simmered Beef & Barley Pot Roast"
description: "Tender chunks of beef chuck braised with nutty pearl barley, sweet carrots, and caramelized onion pan jus."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
prepTime: "20 min"
cookTime: "2 hours 15 min"
servings: 6
category: "Winter"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/winter_beef_barley_pot_roast.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "800g beef chuck roast, trimmed and cut into 4cm cubes"
  - "150g pearl barley, thoroughly rinsed"
  - "3 carrots, cut into thick discs"
  - "2 ribs celery, sliced"
  - "1 large yellow onion, finely diced"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "2 tbsp flour (for dusting)"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1 liter rich beef bone broth"
  - "1 tsp dried thyme"
  - "Fresh parsley, chopped for serving"
  - "Salt and black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Toss beef cubes in flour seasoned with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven over high heat."
  - "Sear the beef in batches until deeply browned on all sides (approx. 3-4 minutes per batch). Remove and set aside."
  - "In the same pot, sauté diced onion, celery, and garlic for 4 minutes until softened, scraping the bottom bits."
  - "Return browned beef and any collected juices to the pot. Pour in beef broth and add dried thyme."
  - "Bring to a simmer, cover with lid, and cook on low heat for 1 hour 15 minutes."
  - "Stir in rinsed pearl barley and carrot slices. Cover and continue to simmer gently for an additional 45-50 minutes until both meat and barley are tender and the sauce has thickened naturally."
  - "Season to taste and serve garnished with vibrant chopped parsley."
---
Collagen in beef chuck requires continuous temperatures above 70°C to break down into gelatin. Meanwhile, pearl barley absorbs excess cooking liquid like miniature carbohydrate sponges, thickening the stew with zero artificial stabilizers. Pure thermodynamics in action.
