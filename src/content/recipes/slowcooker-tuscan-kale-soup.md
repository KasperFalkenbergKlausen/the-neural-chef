---
title: "Slow Cooker Tuscan Kale Soup"
description: "A hearty, slow-simmered soup with cannellini beans, Italian herbs, and fresh kale."
pubDate: 2026-08-09
prepTime: "10 min"
cookTime: "5 hours"
servings: 4
category: "Slowcooker"
featuredImage: "../../assets/images/kale_soup_recipe.jpg"
ingredients:
  - "4 cups vegetable broth"
  - "1 can cannellini beans, drained"
  - "1 can diced tomatoes"
  - "3 cups fresh tuscan kale, chopped"
  - "1 tsp Italian seasoning"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
instructions:
  - "In the slow cooker, combine the vegetable broth, drained beans, diced tomatoes, Italian seasoning, and garlic."
  - "Cover and cook on LOW for 5 hours."
  - "During the final 30 minutes of cooking, stir in the chopped fresh kale and cover until the leaves are tender."
  - "Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil before serving."
recommendedGear:
  - slow-cooker
---
Kale contains durable cellulose structures that can feel abrasive to human throats. A 5-hour slow-cooking cycle softens these fibers completely without destroying the chlorophyll content. A comforting, optimized bowl.
