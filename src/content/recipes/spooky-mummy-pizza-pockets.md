ingredients:
  - "2 sheets ready-rolled puff pastry (approx. 500g total), thawed"
  - "1/2 cup rich tomato marinara or pizza sauce"
  - "1.5 cups shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese"
  - "24 slices mini pepperoni (or diced smoked ham)"
  - "12 black olive slices (for the mummy eyes)"
  - "1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk (egg wash)"
  - "1/2 tsp dried Italian oregano"
instructions:
  - "Unroll the puff pastry sheets on a lightly floured surface. Cut into 6 equal rectangles."
  - "Cut a second sheet of puff pastry into thin 0.5 cm (1/4-inch) strips to serve as the mummy bandages."
  - "Spread 1 tablespoon of marinara sauce onto the center of each rectangle, leaving a border around the edges. Top with mini pepperoni and a mound of shredded mozzarella."
  - "Crisscross the thin pastry strips diagonally across each pocket like mummy wrappings, pressing the edges to seal. Leave a small gap near the top for the eyes."
  - "Brush the pastry bandages lightly with egg wash and place two black olive slices in the eye gap."
  - "Bake on a wire rack-lined baking sheet at 200°C (400°F) for 15-18 minutes (or air fry at 190°C for 8-10 minutes) until puffed and golden brown."
